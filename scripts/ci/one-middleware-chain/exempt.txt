# Routers deliberately served outside the standard middleware chain, each with its one-line reason.
# One per line: <full mount path>\t<reason>. An entry that no longer matches a mount fails.
/health	liveness probe for the platform; no data, no actor
/bullBoard	job dashboard for operators, behind its own basic auth (routes/bullBoard.ts)
/test	test-environment-only routes, mounted under isTest
/api/telemetry/browser	anonymous browser telemetry beacon with its own body limit and per-IP rate limit
/api/internal	service-to-service routes authorized by the shared secret, not a session or token
/api/auth/*	better-auth issues the sessions the chain later reads; it authenticates itself
/api/unsubscribe	one-click unsubscribe authorized by its own signed link, used without a session
